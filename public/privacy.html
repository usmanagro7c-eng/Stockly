Stockly Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Stockly ("the app") is an offline-first stock and inventory manager designed for small shops. This policy explains what data Stockly handles and why.

1. Data stored on your device

The app keeps your business records — purchases, sales, expenses, adjustments, change logs, and settings — in a local database on your own device. This data is stored offline and is never transmitted to us or to any server controlled by the app unless you choose to share it.

2. Google Sheets sync

When you choose to connect a Google Sheet, the app lets Google authenticate your Google account and reads/writes the spreadsheet you select so that your team can collaborate. Only the spreadsheet you explicitly link is accessed. Your records may be stored in that Google Sheet under your own Google account and subject to Google's own privacy terms.

3. Sign-in information

Google sign-in provides us with your name and email address so we can display the connected account in the app. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with any third party.

4. No tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. It has no server of its own for your data.

5. Data deletion

You can delete the app's records at any time by removing the app from your device, which removes the local database. Data in your linked Google Sheet can be deleted by you in that spreadsheet.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact the app developer at the email shown in the Google sign-in consent screen.
